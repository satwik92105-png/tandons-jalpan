Our Story

A Legacy of Authentic Taste Since 1981

For over four decades, Tandon's Jalpan has been synonymous with quality, tradition, and unmatched taste in Patna City. What started as a humble beginning in 1981 has now grown into an iconic food destination loved by generations.

Located in the heart of Sabzi Bazar, Chowk, we take pride in preserving the authentic recipes of our heritage sweets like Gulab Jamun and Rasmalai, while also offering the city's most loved Samosas, crispy Chaat, and a rich variety of North Indian and Chinese delicacies.
